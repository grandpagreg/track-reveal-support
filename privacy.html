Track Reveal Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

What Track Reveal collects

Nothing. Track Reveal has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you.

What stays on your device

Albums you save with the + button are stored only on your iPhone, in the app itself. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

How the app gets its lists

Track Reveal fetches publicly available album and song information from music review publications and from Apple's iTunes Search service to build its lists. Those fetches are how the app shows you new releases and tracks; they are not used to identify you.

Links to other music services

The play buttons open albums and songs in Amazon Music, Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube Music, or Pandora. Once you leave Track Reveal, those services' own privacy policies apply to anything you do there.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here.

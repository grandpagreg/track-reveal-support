Track Reveal Support

New music worth your time, from the review pages that cover it. No account, no subscription.

How it works

Track Reveal gathers new albums, EPs, singles, and standout songs from music review publications, and lets you play them in the music service you already use.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get the latest music?

The app refreshes every time you open it. On the Individual Tracks tab you can also tap Update playlist now to refresh on demand.

How does the playlist download work?

On the Individual Tracks tab, tap Download Playlist (.csv) to save the week's track list as a file. To move it into a music service, upload that file at TuneMyMusic (Select playlists to move → Upload file). You can also copy the plain song list from the same page and paste it wherever you like.

Do the play buttons play music inside Track Reveal?

No. The Amazon Music, Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube Music, and Pandora buttons open the album or song in that service's own app (or website). Track Reveal does not stream music itself.

How do I save an album?

Tap the + button on an album's detail page. Saved albums appear on the Saved tab and stay there until you unsave them.

Why did an album or song disappear?

Lists are kept fresh: new releases and tracks stay for about 2 weeks, Classic albums for about 1 month. Saved albums are exempt and stay until you unsave them.

Which publications does Track Reveal cover?

Pitchfork, Stereogum, Consequence, Aquarium Drunkard, The Quietus, and Hype Machine.

Do you collect my data?

No. Track Reveal has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Your saved albums stay on your device. The app only fetches publicly available feeds to build its lists.
